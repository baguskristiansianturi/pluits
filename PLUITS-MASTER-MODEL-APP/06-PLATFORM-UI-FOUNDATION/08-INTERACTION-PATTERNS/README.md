# 08 — Interaction Patterns

Owns consistent behavior for reusable interactions across the application.

## Register scope

- Navigation, menus, tabs, and disclosure behavior.
- Form input, validation, submission, and recovery.
- Search, filters, sorting, and pagination/infinite loading.
- Dialogs, drawers, popovers, confirmation, and dismissal.
- Loading, empty, error, success, retry, and disabled states.
- Keyboard, focus, pointer, touch, and reduced-motion behavior.

## Rules

- A component's behavior must be specified alongside its visual appearance.
- Use consistent feedback and state transitions for equivalent actions.
- Do not rely on color alone to communicate state.
- Preserve keyboard access, visible focus, touch usability, and appropriate reduced-motion behavior.
- Domain-specific workflows remain in their owning Product Design and ecosystem specifications.
