## Current design baseline
- PLUITS Store is a merchant vertical connected to the PLUITS Marketplace.
- Sellers require a verifiable physical store/business premises and PLUITS approval.
- Customer homepage aggregates products from eligible stores; each seller has its own profile/catalog and management module.
- Product catalog supports category-specific attributes and multiple product types, not one generic form.
- Marketplace and POS must use authoritative shared inventory.
- Local and global ambition is subject to country-specific readiness.

## Constraints
- This is not a separate standalone product.
- Keep work isolated while designed and built.
- Preserve existing PLUITS canonical URLs and navigation unless explicitly approved otherwise.
- Treat PROPOSED and OPEN decisions as not final.
