# PLUITS Profile Identity

**Status:** PROPOSED — founder clarification recorded; exact visual assets and styling remain open.  
**Scope:** Visual recognition of membership level on the user's profile.

## Confirmed direction from founder discussion

Every PLUITS membership level uses the **same profile page layout and the same profile content structure**.

The following are not membership-level layout variants: profile sections, profile information, feed, posts, photos, videos, Reels, tabs, navigation, and the general arrangement of the page. They remain consistent across Regular, Silver, Gold, Platinum, and Golden PLUITS Priority.

The purpose of the membership distinction is simply to let people recognize which membership level an account currently has.

## The only intended profile-level differences

Membership identity may be expressed through limited visual details, such as:
- A membership icon or badge.
- A small membership-specific background treatment or accent.
- A consistent visual treatment associated with that level.

The exact icon artwork, background area, colors, and placement are not finalized. They should be discussed and selected later. Do not expand these visual differences into different profile layouts or different profile content.

## Membership and verification are separate

- Membership identity indicates the current membership level.
- Verification identity indicates a separate verification status.
- A Regular account may be verified.
- A paid membership does not automatically mean the account is verified.

These indicators must not be conflated.

## Relationship to account permissions

Differences in what a member can do belong to account rules, permissions, and account settings—not to a different profile layout. For example, premium-only capabilities may be available in settings or feature flows according to the agreed membership entitlements. The profile itself retains the common structure.

## Future design and implementation notes

When this concept is ready for implementation, define:
1. Which profile element carries the membership identity.
2. The icon/badge design for each level.
3. Whether a small background or accent treatment is used, and exactly where.
4. How current membership status is obtained and rendered consistently.
5. Accessible labels and fallback behavior so membership is recognizable without relying on color alone.
6. How membership expiry or change updates the visual identity.

Do not invent different profile sections, feed behavior, content visibility, or navigation by membership level in this document. Those require separate product rules and must not be implied by the visual identity.

## Open design decisions

- Final icon/symbol family for Regular, Silver, Gold, Platinum, and Golden PLUITS Priority.
- Exact background/accent treatment and placement.
- Whether Regular uses a neutral identifier or simply the standard appearance.
- Exact behavior during membership expiry, renewal, or transition.

No frontend implementation or visual assets are approved by this concept note.
