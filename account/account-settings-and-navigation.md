# PLUITS Account Settings and Navigation

**Status:** PROPOSED — working area for ongoing founder discussion.  
**Scope:** What account settings manage and how account-related controls may be grouped and navigated.

## Purpose

This document defines the responsibilities of account settings and, later, the menu/navigation structure that exposes those controls. It is not a final menu design. Names, grouping, order, and navigation may be rearranged whenever discussion identifies a clearer and more useful structure.

## Product questions to resolve

For each setting or control, document:
- What the setting is for and which user problem it solves.
- Which account levels can access it.
- Whether it is a basic account control or a membership entitlement.
- What the available states and choices are.
- What happens when the setting changes.
- Which frontend screens and feedback are needed.
- Which backend permission checks, data, APIs, and security rules are required.
- Dependencies, edge cases, and test scenarios.

## Candidate responsibility areas (not a final menu)

These are topic areas to investigate, not a commitment to final labels or order:
- Account identity and profile details.
- Login, authentication, and account recovery.
- Privacy, audience, and discoverability.
- Social relationships and interaction controls.
- Notifications and communication preferences.
- Membership status, benefits, and entitlement information.
- Safety, blocking, reporting, and restrictions.
- Sessions, devices, and security.
- Accessibility and general preferences.

## Flexible information architecture

Menu names, nesting, order, and grouping are intentionally open. Reorganize them based on user understanding, task frequency, safety, and consistency. Do not treat the first proposed arrangement as the baseline that must be retained.

## Rule

A menu item must represent a clear user task or setting responsibility. Do not create separate profile layouts for membership tiers. Membership differences should appear as permissions, available controls, or feature behavior where relevant.
