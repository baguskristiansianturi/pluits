# PLUITS Social — Local Radio Live Network and Interactive Broadcasts

- Date: 2026-11-27
- Status: UNDER-REVIEW
- Area: Social Media / Live / Local Discovery / Partnerships
- Source: User product-sharing discussion
- Scope: Product concept capture only. No implementation or deployment authorized.

## 1. User's concept and intent

PLUITS should offer a Live experience alongside Blog and Feed that connects local radio stations to PLUITS audiences. This is not merely a generic livestream feature or a plan to replace radio. The strategic idea is to modernize and extend existing local radio broadcasts through a digital distribution and interaction channel in PLUITS.

The user gave Bali and Jakarta as examples:
- A listener can discover local radio stations in Bali.
- The listener can change the region and explore stations in Jakarta or other locations.
- A participating station continues its regular broadcast on local radio while its live session is also presented and heard through PLUITS.
- The station's live session appears in the PLUITS Live viewing experience/player (the user referred to a screen/player experience that is to be made).
- PLUITS should make the origin of the experience recognizable, so listeners associate the digital radio experience with PLUITS even when the station's broadcast is also heard on conventional radio.

The intended strategic benefit is mutual: radio stations gain digital reach and a new audience interaction channel; PLUITS gains local content, recurring audience attention, and brand awareness through partner broadcasts.

## 2. Two related live experiences

### A. Local Radio Live

A discoverable directory or feed of live radio stations, organized by geographic area:
- Explore stations around Bali or another selected region.
- Switch to Jakarta or any other available region.
- Open a station's active live broadcast and listen inside PLUITS.
- Display station identity and relevant live-program/session information.
- Support discovery of participating stations rather than presenting only a generic list of livestream creators.

The concept is geographic discovery, not a restriction that listeners must physically be in the broadcast region.

### B. Interactive Radio Live

Listeners can participate while the radio program is on air:
- Send greetings, shout-outs, requests, or short messages/attention for the presenter.
- Presenters can select and read listener submissions on air.
- For selected programs, a presenter may invite a listener into a live video interaction.
- During that video interaction, the listener's own video can appear in a small picture-in-picture window while the main broadcast remains visible, similar to a video-call layout.

These are complementary modes. A standard radio session can remain audio-first; video participation is an optional program format, not a requirement for every broadcast.

## 3. Account eligibility and participation

The user stated that PLUITS users with at least a Verified account should be able to create attention/participation around these broadcasts. The precise permission model is not yet defined.

Potential distinction to preserve for later product design:
- Listening and browsing may be open more broadly.
- Sending on-air messages, greetings, requests, or joining a video segment may require a verified account, subject to station/program rules.
- The station and presenter should retain editorial control over which messages are read and who is invited on camera.

Do not interpret this as approval to require verification for passive listening; that was not specified.

## 4. Partnership and brand strategy

The concept depends on working with local radio stations rather than simply rebroadcasting arbitrary radio streams.

Potential partnership value:
- Radio stations bring existing presenters, programming, local trust, and conventional broadcast audiences.
- PLUITS supplies a digital live destination, audience interaction tools, geographic discovery, and an additional way to reach listeners.
- The PLUITS name and identity should be visible and consistently associated with the PLUITS digital experience.
- A listener who first discovers a program through PLUITS may remember PLUITS as the platform that connected them to that station.

The user described this as a strategic way to modernize local radio and help PLUITS become familiar through repeated exposure and participation.

## 5. Product principles to preserve

- This is a partnership-led local radio network, not a proposal to build a radio station or replace existing broadcasters.
- Keep conventional radio broadcast and PLUITS digital distribution conceptually distinct, while allowing them to carry the same live program when the partnership permits.
- Local discovery should let users deliberately change regions; it should not be locked to the user's current location.
- Keep the listening experience simple and reliable. Video-call-style listener participation is an optional layer.
- PLUITS should be recognizable in the digital player and interaction experience without misrepresenting who owns or produces the radio program.
- Radio presenters/stations need control of the live program, message selection, and guest invitations.

## 6. Open questions and feasibility checks

No partnership, licensing, technical, or commercial arrangement has been confirmed. Before launch, investigate:
- Which radio stations are interested and what partnership model they would accept.
- Permission to simulcast or stream the station's broadcast online, including music and other third-party rights. A station's terrestrial broadcast permissions may not automatically cover internet streaming or a third-party platform.
- Whether PLUITS receives an authorized stream directly from the station, uses a station-managed encoder, or uses another agreed technical integration.
- Audio quality, latency, stream uptime, peak concurrent listeners, bandwidth/CDN costs, and fallback behavior.
- How to represent station, program, presenter, city/region, live status, schedule, and replay availability (if any).
- Whether greetings/requests are moderated before being shown to presenters, how abuse/spam is handled, and whether messages are retained.
- Consent and safety rules for live video guests, including invitation, camera/microphone permissions, moderation, age safeguards, recording, and removal.
- Whether a listener's video/audio contribution can be included in a broadcast distributed through conventional radio; this may require separate production and consent arrangements.
- Commercial model: partner revenue share, sponsorship, advertising, listener support, or other options. No revenue split is decided.
- Brand presentation and attribution: how PLUITS and the radio station are both credited accurately.

## 7. Decisions versus proposals

### User-stated direction
- PLUITS should have a Live experience alongside Blog and Feed.
- The Live concept should include local radio stations and allow discovery by region, with Bali and Jakarta as examples.
- Participating stations' programs should be available live through PLUITS while the stations also continue their conventional radio broadcasts.
- Listeners should be able to send greetings/attention/messages for presenters to read.
- Selected sessions may include video participation, with the listener shown in a small picture-in-picture window.
- The strategy is to modernize local radio, help stations reach audiences digitally, and make PLUITS recognizable through the experience.
- Users with at least a Verified account were mentioned as able to create participation/attention; exact access rules remain open.

### Not yet decided
- Partner stations, launch cities, agreements, rights, streaming architecture, UI details, moderation policy, video guest rules, monetization, and launch sequence.
- Whether the feature should include archived/replay programs.
- Whether the on-air attention feature is text-only, voice notes, or both.
- Exact eligibility and limits by account level.

## 8. Guardrails

- Do not rebroadcast a station or music without the necessary online distribution rights and permissions.
- Do not imply a station has partnered with PLUITS until an agreement exists.
- Do not promise that a listener's video appearance will be broadcast over terrestrial radio without explicit production and consent arrangements.
- Do not turn tier status into a way to bypass moderation or force presenters to read a user's message.
- Keep this session UNDER-REVIEW until partnership, rights, user safety, and cost feasibility have been assessed.
